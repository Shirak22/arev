Third-Party Services

The Arev extension uses the following third-party services:

Open-Meteo Weather API

Service: open-meteo.com
Purpose: Weather forecast data
License: Free for non-commercial use (see their Terms of Service)

ip-api.com

Service: ip-api.com
Purpose: IP-based geolocation
License: Free tier available (see their Terms of Service)

BigDataCloud Reverse Geocoding API

Service: bigdatacloud.com
Purpose: Reverse geocoding (coordinates to city/country names)
License: Free tier available (see their Terms of Service)
