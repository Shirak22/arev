About Arev Tab Extension

Version:1.0.0

Welcome to Arev—a beautifully simple weather extension that transforms your new tab into a clean, elegant, and informative experience. Unlike cluttered extensions that overwhelm you with unnecessary features, Arev embraces minimalism while delivering everything you need at a glance.

Why Choose Arev?

Most new tab extensions are crowded with widgets, ads, and distractions. Arev is different. We believe your new tab should be a breath of fresh air—literally and figuratively. Our extension is completely free, requires no sign-up, and respects your privacy by keeping all data stored locally on your device.

Simple. Clean. Effective. That's our promise. Every time you open a new tab, you'll see exactly what matters: the weather, beautifully presented without any clutter.

Key Features

Real-Time Weather Information

Get instant access to current weather conditions and a detailed 3-day forecast right on your new tab. See temperature, conditions, and essential weather metrics at a glance—no need to navigate away or open another app.

Dynamic Weather-Matched Backgrounds

Experience the weather visually with our dynamic background system. Each new tab automatically displays a stunning background that perfectly matches your current weather conditions. Sunny skies bring bright, cheerful scenes; rainy days show cozy, atmospheric imagery; snowy weather displays crisp winter landscapes. The background adapts in real-time, creating an immersive experience that connects you with the world outside.

This isn't just decoration—it's a visual cue that helps you instantly understand the weather before you even read the numbers. Your new tab becomes a window to the weather, not just a data display.

Smart Clothing Recommendations

Never wonder what to wear again. Arev provides personalized clothing recommendations based on the current weather conditions. Whether it's suggesting a light jacket for a breezy day, reminding you to grab an umbrella for rain, or recommending layers for cold weather, we help you dress appropriately for the conditions.

This feature is designed to make your life easier. Instead of checking multiple weather apps and trying to interpret temperature readings, Arev gives you actionable advice right when you need it. Start your day prepared, comfortable, and confident about your outfit choice.

The Arev Philosophy

We built Arev because we were tired of extensions that promised simplicity but delivered complexity. Our design philosophy is built on three principles:

  • Minimalism: Every element serves a purpose. No unnecessary features, no visual clutter.
  • Beauty: Weather information should be presented beautifully, not just functionally.
  • Effectiveness: You get the information you need, when you need it, without any friction.

Privacy First

Your privacy matters. Arev stores all data locally on your device using Chrome's local storage. We don't collect personal information, we don't track your browsing, and we don't sell your data. The extension works entirely client-side, ensuring your information stays with you.

Get Started

Using Arev is effortless. Simply install the extension and open a new tab. The extension will automatically detect your location (with your permission) and display your local weather. No configuration needed, no accounts to create, no settings to tweak—just open and enjoy.

If you want to customize your experience, you can access settings to clear cache, adjust location preferences, or manage data. But even without touching any settings, Arev works beautifully out of the box.
